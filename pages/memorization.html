Script notes

  • L1 (The Consonants, p. 21): Five letters have special word-final forms: ך, ם, ן, ף, ץ.
  • L3 (שְׁוָא (Shava), p. 29): Under gutturals, שְׁוָא is commonly replaced by a composite sheva: חֲטַף־פַּתַח, חֲטַף־סֶגוֹל, or חֲטַף־קָמֶץ.

Demonstratives

Demonstrative pronouns — L33 (Demonstrative Pronouns, p. 96)
SetHebrew (as printed)
Nearזֶה (MS), זֹאת (FS), אֵלֶּה (plural)

Quoted from the source concepts: “Near forms are זֶה (MS), זֹאת (FS), and אֵלֶּה (plural).” “Far demonstratives are identical to third-person personal pronouns: הוּא, הִיא, הֵמָּה, and הֵנָּה.”
