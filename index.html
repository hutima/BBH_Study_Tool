User guide

How to use this app

An unofficial, student-made study aid for John A. Cook & Robert D. Holmstedt, Beginning Biblical Hebrew: A Grammar and Illustrated Reader (Baker Academic) — not affiliated with or endorsed by the authors or publisher. Four study modes (Vocabulary, Parsing, Grammar, Reader) all run locally in your browser; nothing you study or mark is sent anywhere unless you explicitly export it as a JSON file yourself.

Reader passages: Hebrew text and morphology from the Open Scriptures Hebrew Bible Project (https://github.com/openscriptures/morphhb), OSHB version 2.2 (v.2.2). Lemma and morphology data licensed under CC BY 4.0.

Home screen

Choose session sets what to study — a lesson range, or individual lessons; it also has the Lesson 0 · Alphabet entry, set apart above the lesson list. Start studying opens or resumes the current selection. The Vocab / Parsing / Grammar / Reader buttons switch modes. Progress opens analytics. Reference opens the paradigm/vocabulary reference page. User guide is this screen.

Vocabulary mode

Flip a card, mark how confident you felt, and the app schedules what comes back next. Study a single lesson or a range via Choose session — the decade ranges (1–10 … 41–50) or the textbook’s own 13 “Unit” reading-block presets. Lesson 0 · Alphabet, above the lesson list, is a separate flip-card deck for the 23 Hebrew letters — it has its own Again/Got it marking and progress line, and never touches your vocabulary deck, SRS, XP, streaks, or export vocab counts.

Every card carries its own schedule; how you mark it sets when it returns:

  • ✗ Hard — back within minutes.
  • ~ Uncertain — back at about half the previous interval.
  • ✓ Easy — advances along ~1 → 3 → 7 → 14 days and onward, faster the more accurately you’ve been answering.

Cards flow through three lanes — active (due now), middle (just missed), and deferred (a future day); the bottom panel’s Cards left mirrors this. ↶ Undo reverses your last mark, and a lapse on a known card runs a short relearn ladder rather than resetting it. With Spaced review off it’s a plain flip deck: Easy archives a card, Hard/Uncertain send it to the back, and ↻ Reset restores the archived cards.

Parsing mode

Drills morphological analysis — binyan/stem, conjugation, person/gender/number, state, and pronominal suffixes — gated so nothing beyond your current lesson appears. Pick Parse (given a form, identify it), Build (given a target analysis, choose the matching form), or Mixed. Scope the pool with Focused paradigm, Shuffle all, a Custom set of chosen paradigms, Exclude known, and an Appendix forms toggle (off by default) for material the textbook only prints in an appendix. A form counts as known once you’ve gotten it right two attempts in a row under whichever dimensions are currently graded.

Grammar mode

Short multiple-choice questions on grammar concepts, gated to your current lesson so nothing ahead of where you are shows up. Turn on Review missed to focus on questions you’ve gotten wrong before, and switch Difficulty between All questions and Core-only.

Reader mode

Curated Hebrew Bible passages, gated by lesson and split into Strict (only vocabulary already introduced) and Guided (a few not-yet-introduced words, underlined) tiers. Tap any word for its lemma, morphology, and gate/vocab lesson; tap ☆ Mark for review to flag it for yourself — a plain personal note, never scheduled or graded. Reader has no SRS of any kind.

Settings & tools

Under Advanced review settings (vocabulary mode only; all persist; each toggle has an (i) for details):

  • Shuffle, English → Hebrew (flip direction), and Spaced review (SRS on/off, default on).
  • Hard review — only the stubborn vocab you keep missing at low confidence.
  • Pace — relaxed 8-month (default) or 2-month intensive, chosen on first launch and switchable here.
  • Display — Serif/Sans, three text sizes, and Vowel points (pointed/unpointed) and Transliteration (show/hide) for Hebrew card faces. These display toggles never affect Lesson 0 Alphabet or Reader, which always show pointed text.
  • Reset actions (own section) reshuffle, reset a deck, or wipe all stats; Progress tools export/import progress as JSON — everything stays on this device unless you choose to export it.

Study aid notice re-opens the disclaimer; Contact author (the footer link, or below) opens a card for feedback, corrections, or support.

Keyboard (while studying vocabulary)

Flip Space/Enter · move ← → · mark 1 Hard / 2 Uncertain / 3 Easy · theme is top-right of the header.

Before you begin

Please read before using this app

This study tool was made with AI by a student. It is unofficial and may contain mistakes, omissions, or misleading answers.

Always check vocabulary against your course content, instructor guidance, and official materials.

This app is meant to help with review and memorization, not to replace assigned coursework or authoritative sources.

Your progress stays on this device (browser local storage) and is never sent anywhere — it leaves only if you choose to export it yourself as a JSON file.

Quick start: flip a card, then mark Hard, Uncertain, or Easy.

Choose your study pace
You can change this anytime in Advanced settings.
Reset spaced review

What would you like to reset?

Set all to now keeps your SRS progress (streaks, intervals, ease) but makes every card in scope due immediately, so you can review them right away.

Smooth schedule keeps all progress and just levels the review calendar: when many cards pile up on the same future day (e.g. after completing a reset), it pulls some of them to earlier days so roughly the same number come due each day. Cards due within the next 3 days are left alone, and nothing is ever pushed later.

Reset progress clears all SRS scheduling and progress for the cards in scope. Pass history and unspaced marks are kept.

Reset stats

What would you like to reset?

Reset stats clears all saved study stats, marks, spaced-review scheduling, achievements, and study-time history. Your settings (theme, fonts, profile, disclaimer) are kept.

Reset to start wipes everything and returns the app to its initial launch state — stats, settings, theme, fonts, profile, and the study-aid disclaimer are all cleared, and the page will reload.

Reset deck

Reset this flip deck?

Brings every archived card back into rotation and reshuffles the deck. Your spaced-review scheduling, intervals, confidence history, and pass history are kept.
