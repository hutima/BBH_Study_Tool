Irregular practice

Stem-change flashcards for irregular verbs — flip between the present and its aorist, future, or perfect, with the letters that change highlighted. Grouped here rather than by chapter since they span the course.

Paradigm practice

Extra paradigm and vocabulary sets, grouped by the Duff chapter where each is taught. Pick a whole chapter or an individual paradigm to add it to your study deck.

Advanced vocabulary

Rare New Testament Greek lemmas not covered in your course, ordered by descending NT frequency in buckets of 200 (sub‑groups of 25). These are not counted toward course‑wide progress, but they do appear in the analytics for whatever you have selected.

NT Book Vocab

Every lexeme that appears in each book of the New Testament, grouped into sets of 50 by how often it occurs in that book. These are not new cards — each links to its existing card, so studying a book’s vocabulary shares progress with that word’s home chapter or advanced bucket. Lexeme–book frequencies are drawn from the SBLGNT.

User guide

How to use this app

An unofficial study aid for Duff's Elementary New Testament Greek. Flip cards, mark how confident you felt, and the app schedules what comes back next.

Changelog
Build mode & parsing depth · 2026-06
  • Build mode. An interactive paradigm reference — pick a paradigm and conjugate or decline it one part at a time (tense → voice → mood → person, or case → number → gender). It covers every legitimate form, offers look-alike forms down either the middle or passive branch, and isn’t chapter-gated.
  • Voice & gender steps. Voice enters at Ch. 14/15; deponents (…μαι) now parse as a single middle, with a note on the contested ‘deponent’ label, instead of also accepting active. Single-gender nouns get a one-tap gender step that names the noun type, and combined options (neuter nominative/accusative, two-termination genders) sit right next to the simple cases. On multi-value case, gender, and aspect forms, naming just one value (e.g. nominative) now earns partial credit — accepted (marked with a yellow †) and kept in rotation, but the form isn’t counted as mastered until you name the full set. Undoing a correct answer and re-confirming it still counts correct (flagged with a *); ending on a wrong pick is still marked wrong. Plus fuller paradigm coverage across moods and voices.
  • Optative mood (Ch. 20). The rare optative now parses and conjugates in Build mode. Only the parses that actually turn up in the NT are required to memorise (Paul’s μὴ γένοιτο, εἴη, δῴη), each mirrored onto the model λύω / ῥύομαι paradigms so you drill the same slots on familiar verbs; the full optative tables ride along in the optional paradigm pool.
  • Parsing progress at a glance. The review panel now lists every in-scope paradigm — including ones you haven’t drilled yet — so you can see the whole deck and how much you’ve covered (“X drilled · Y in scope”).
  • Drill any paradigm in Parsing. From the paradigm dropdown, drill one focused paradigm, pool all paradigms through the selected chapter shuffled together, or hand-pick a Custom paradigm set.
  • Variant forms as cards — second aorist, liquid future, aorist passive, perfect active, and μι-verbs, each as its own card once its chapter is in scope. A shared word only moves on once all its forms are cleared Easy together in one 2-hour round. As you answer each form it drops out of “due now”; Hard just sends a form back through the queue, while any form you mark Uncertain resets the whole set so you re-try them all — so the word advances (~1 day) only when every form is cleared in a single pass.
  • Gentler spaced lapses. Missing a well-known card no longer resets it: a slip runs a short relearn ladder (Uncertain re-tests in 2 h, Hard over a day or two), then resumes at half its old spacing. The 8-month pace now grows more evenly (capped near 2 months) and drills a card that keeps lapsing.
  • Pick your pace on first launch. New users choose between the relaxed 8-month pace (now the default) and the 2-month intensive course pace right from the opening screen — switchable anytime in Advanced settings.
  • Undo a parse guess for partial credit, so you can re-pick and keep practising the right path.
  • Tidier settings. “Required only” is now Starred words only (Duff’s starred vocab), reachable from both Advanced settings and the session picker; Reset actions are now their own top-level section, and Progress tools (export/import) sit in an always-open frame in Advanced settings.
  • Cleaner session picker. The old course-supplement sets were merged away: duplicate supplemental vocab was folded back into the regular chapters (so each word appears once), every chapter now uses the same group layout, and the legacy lecture-week grammar rides along with the matching paradigm (or its chapter) instead of a separate supplemental. Each week’s two halves are now a single Part N.
  • Greek alphabet deck. A new Chapter 0 / Part 0 drills the alphabet — each letter with its name spelt out (α / αλφα → a / alpha). It’s kept out of the Mid-Term / Final Exam select-alls since the letters aren’t vocabulary.

Home screen

Choose session sets what to study — pre-built sessions, individual chapters (Chapter 0 is the alphabet), per-chapter paradigm-practice decks, NT-book vocab, or Advanced rare-lemma buckets. Start studying opens or resumes the current selection.

The mode strip switches card type (or opens a separate page):

  • Vocab — Greek headword ↔ English gloss.
  • Grammar — multiple-choice form and parsing drills from the chapter paradigms.
  • Parsing — parse a form one dimension at a time (aspect → tense → voice → mood → person → number → case → gender). It has its own Current chapter dropdown; drill a focused paradigm, all paradigms through the chapter, or a custom set, and tune dimensions under Parsing options. Switch the chapter dropdown to Build mode for an untimed conjugate/decline reference.
  • Reader — Textus Receptus verses unlocked by chapter, plus gentler per-chapter Translation drills; tap a verse for a literal English line.
  • Paradigms — a separate page of full reference tables; the toolbar can hide Greek, English, or notes for self-testing.

Progress opens analytics (streak, level, heatmap, completion bars, achievements, study time, per-deck stats). User guide is this screen.

How spaced repetition works

Every card carries its own schedule; how you mark it sets when it returns:

  • ✗ Hard — back within minutes.
  • ~ Uncertain — back at about half the previous interval.
  • ✓ Easy — advances along ~1 → 3 → 7 → 14 days and onward, faster the more accurately you’ve been answering.

Cards flow through three lanes — active (due now), middle (just missed), and deferred (a future day); the bottom panel’s Cards left mirrors this. ↶ Undo reverses your last mark, and a lapse on a known card runs a short relearn ladder rather than resetting it. With Spaced review off it’s a plain flip deck: Easy archives a card, Hard/Uncertain send it to the back, and ↻ Reset restores the archived cards.

Settings & tools

Under Advanced review settings (all persist; each toggle has an (i) for details):

  • Shuffle, Eng → Gk (flip direction), and Spaced review (SRS on/off, default on).
  • Starred words only — just Duff’s starred vocab (default on; also in the session picker).
  • Hard review — only the stubborn vocab you keep missing at low confidence.
  • Variant forms as cards — drill a verb’s aorist / future / perfect / aorist-passive / μι forms as their own cards once their chapter is in scope.
  • Parsing options — per-dimension toggles, per-value excludes, and an Optional paradigm pool (subjunctive, optative, infinitives, participles…).
  • Pace — relaxed 8-month (default) or 2-month intensive, chosen on first launch and switchable here.
  • Display — Serif/Sans and three text sizes.
  • Reset actions (own section) reshuffle, reset a deck, reset starred, or wipe all stats; Progress tools export/import progress as JSON.

This study tool was made with AI by a student. It is unofficial and may contain mistakes, omissions, or misleading answers.

Always check vocabulary, parsing, grammar explanations, and quiz content against your course content, instructor guidance, and official materials.

This app is meant to help with review and memorization, not to replace assigned coursework or authoritative sources.

Reset spaced review

What would you like to reset?

Set all to now keeps your SRS progress (streaks, intervals, ease) but makes every card in scope due immediately, so you can review them right away.

Smooth schedule keeps all progress and just levels the review calendar: when many cards pile up on the same future day (e.g. after completing a reset), it pulls some of them to earlier days so roughly the same number come due each day. Cards due within the next 3 days are left alone, and nothing is ever pushed later.

Reset progress clears all SRS scheduling and progress for the cards in scope. Pass history and unspaced marks are kept.

Reset stats

What would you like to reset?

Reset stats clears all saved study stats, marks, spaced-review scheduling, achievements, and study-time history. Your settings (theme, fonts, profile, disclaimer) are kept.

Reset to start wipes everything and returns the app to its initial launch state — stats, settings, theme, fonts, profile, and the study-aid disclaimer are all cleared, and the page will reload.

Reset deck

Reset this flip deck?

Brings every archived card back into rotation and reshuffles the deck. Your spaced-review scheduling, intervals, confidence history, and pass history are kept.

Reset known

What would you like to reset?

Sets each form's per-form tally back to 0/2, so its parsing dots read as unseen again and any forms hidden by “Exclude known morphs” come back into the drill. Per-paradigm rolling % and history are kept.

Current paradigm only clears just the current paradigm.

All paradigms clears every paradigm you've drilled.

Parsing

Aspect step is now off by default

The parsing walk no longer asks a separate Aspect step (continuous / undefined / completed) before Tense — aspect is derivable from tense, so this shortens each walk by one step.

Want it back? Open Advanced settings → Parsing options and switch Aspect step (parsing) on.
